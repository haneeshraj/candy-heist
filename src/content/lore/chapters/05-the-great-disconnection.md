---
title: The Great Disconnection
line: Intelligence itself works like interference.
state: disconnect
---

As civilization advanced, technology accelerated, urbanization expanded, artificial systems emerged and individual identity strengthened.

Over time, intelligent life grew increasingly disconnected from Omun. The more advanced civilization became, the less naturally synchronized it became.

> Intelligence itself functions almost like interference within the planetary signal.

Modern citizens know Omun exists. Few understand what it truly is. Most have forgotten what it once meant.
