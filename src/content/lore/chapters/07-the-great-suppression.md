Millions of years before the present day, civilization discovered truths about Omun that fundamentally changed society.

> A decision was made. The truth would not be destroyed. It would be controlled.

Knowledge surrounding Omun, Nayarasam, resonance manipulation and collective consciousness became centralized and restricted.

History was gradually rewritten. Public understanding diminished. The original purpose of Omun was buried beneath countless generations of information control.
