Long ago, researchers dedicated themselves to studying Omun and the resonance frequencies associated with it. Their discipline became known as Sonoalchemy: the study and application of harmonic frequencies to influence consciousness, memory, emotion and biological resonance.

It combines:

- Sound
- Frequency manipulation
- Consciousness research
- Biology
- Chemistry
- Emotional synchronization

Through it, practitioners learned to influence memory, emotion, perception, consciousness and collective behavior. It occupies a space between science, ritual, technology, art and spiritual practice.

> Sound is not heard. It is structured. It is shaped. It becomes experience.

Its mark, a four-pointed star in a ring, stands for the convergence of sound, consciousness and structure: frequency waves, the circle of consciousness, structure and order, and their application.
