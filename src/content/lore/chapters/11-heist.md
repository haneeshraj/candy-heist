At the center of the story is a rogue practitioner of Sonoalchemy known as Heist. He uses sound, resonance, underground gatherings and forbidden techniques to awaken fragments of forgotten truth within the population.

> His events are not concerts. They are acts of remembrance.

Participants leave with:

- Forgotten emotions
- Missing memories
- Questions they should not possess
- Glimpses of realities hidden by society

To some, Heist is a healer. To others, a terrorist. To the planetary authority, he is a threat capable of destabilizing the foundation of civilization itself.

The name Candy Heist refers not to the theft of material wealth, but to the theft of artificial transcendence, forbidden memory and collective awakening, from a civilization that has forgotten what it lost.
