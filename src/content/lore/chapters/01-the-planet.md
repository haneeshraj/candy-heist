Nayara is a colossal living planet, vastly larger than Earth, holding an immense diversity of environments, ecosystems, climates and geological formations.

Its scale let life evolve independently across enormous distances for millions of years, into countless unique biomes and evolutionary pathways.

- Crystalline deserts
- Planet-spanning fungal forests
- Floating ecosystems
- Living geological structures
- Resonant caverns
- Bioluminescent oceans
- Megaflora canopies
- Organic megastructures

> Nayara is not merely inhabited by life. Nayara itself is the lifeform.

Every ecosystem, organism and civilization functions as part of a larger planetary superorganism. Forests behave like lungs. Oceans behave like circulatory systems. Fungal networks resemble neural pathways.

The entire planet operates as a biological consciousness, on a planetary scale.
