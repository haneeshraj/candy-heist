The planetary authority holds advanced resonance knowledge inherited from ancient Sonoalchemy. Rather than relying on traditional oppression, it manipulates collective memory.

Individuals who discover forbidden truths are often erased from public perception. Records disappear. Memories fragment. Relationships dissolve.

People remember absences without remembering what is missing. History itself can be edited through resonance.

> The greatest weapon in society is forgetting.
