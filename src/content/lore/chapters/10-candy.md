Within underground culture, artificially induced fragments of Omun are known as Candy. Candy is not necessarily a single substance: it is a cultural term for experiences that temporarily reconnect individuals to collective consciousness.

These experiences may involve sound, frequencies, ritual, psychoactive compounds or resonance technologies.

Those who take it often report:

- Shared memories
- Collective emotions
- Heightened empathy
- Planetary visions
- Temporary dissolution of self

> Whether these experiences are genuine reconnections or sophisticated hallucinations remains unknown.
